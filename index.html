Questions before you ask

Short answers.

Who is COVERLIST for?

Independent supper clubs, pop-ups, private chefs, chef residencies, tasting events and other small-capacity food experiences that want a better direct booking flow.

Do you take a percentage of bookings?

No. The starting offer is £450 to launch and £49/month for hosting and upkeep, with 0% COVERLIST per-booking commission.

Does the showcase take real payments?

No. AFTER THE SPIN is deliberately labelled as a demo. For a real build, payment requirements are scoped to the operator’s actual setup before anything is promised.

Can it look like my existing brand?

That is the point. Each public booking surface is designed around the operator rather than forcing every dinner into the same marketplace template.

What happens after I enquire?

You send your name, project and contact email. We review the current booking setup and reply with a concrete recommendation and exact scope before any work starts.
